# Scorper Creative templates (studied 2026-10-18)

The previews were studied from Marcus's logged-in Scorper dashboard. No Scorper files are kept in this repo; every Hermes template is drawn from scratch to match the look. Each one has an image slot that takes an upload or a generated image.

Other modes seen in the Loom:
- Screenshot: tweet card with avatar, name, verified badge, handle, and text. Light, Dark and Black backgrounds.
- Photo: Split (two photos stacked, text on each), Single, Hook, Before/After (two columns with a center divider), Started vs Going.
- Editorial: Cover (photo with a gradient, circle inset, rule plus brand badge, condensed caps headline) and Story.
- Clone: one cover upload plus up to 3 inner slide uploads.
